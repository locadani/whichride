# Store listing: English (en-US)

**App name** (30): whichride

**Short description** (80):
Which Berlin carsharing is cheapest for this trip? Share a Maps link and see.

**Full description** (4000):

Got a Google Maps link for where you're going? Share it to whichride and see what the trip costs with each carsharing service in Berlin, cheapest first.

• Share a place from Google Maps, paste a link or type an address
• Prices for MILES, Bolt Drive, SIXT share and more, per car or size class
• Your own discounts and passes are included in the price
• Not sure of the distance? Enter kilometres and minutes yourself
• One tap opens the provider's app or the route in Google Maps

Prices come from users. When a price changes, anyone can report it and others confirm it, so the list stays up to date without anyone in charge.

Your trips stay on your phone. whichride stores no routes or locations: they are used to work out the distance and then forgotten.

whichride is an independent project and is not affiliated with any carsharing provider.
